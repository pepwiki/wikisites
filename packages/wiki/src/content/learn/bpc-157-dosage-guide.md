## BPC-157 Dosage Guide

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a 15-amino acid peptide derived from human gastric juice. It demonstrates broad tissue-protective effects in preclinical models, including accelerate wound healing, gastrointestinal repair, and musculoskeletal recovery. No FDA-approved clinical indications exist; all dosing data derives from animal studies and preclinical research.

## Molecular Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Sequence | GEPPPGKPADDAGLV |
| Molecular weight | 1,419.5 Da |
| Stability | Resistant to gastric acid (oral bioavailable) |
| Half-life | ~4 hours (estimated) |
| Solubility | Water-soluble |

## Route-Dependent Dosing

BPC-157 exhibits different pharmacokinetic profiles depending on administration route:

### Oral Administration

Oral dosing exploits BPC-157's gastric acid stability. The peptide survives first-pass metabolism and exerts local GI effects before systemic absorption.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Bioavailability | Estimated 50–70% (GI local effects不受 first-pass影响) |
| Onset | 15–30 minutes |
| Duration | 4–6 hours |
| Frequency | 1–2 times daily |

**Typical research doses:**

| Indication | Dose Range | Frequency |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| GI protection | 10–50 µg/kg | 1–2× daily |
| Wound healing | 10–20 µg/kg | 1–2× daily |
| Musculoskeletal | 10–20 µg/kg | 1–2× daily |
| Neuroprotection | 10–20 µg/kg | 1–2× daily |

### Injectable Administration (Subcutaneous)

Subcutaneous injection provides higher systemic bioavailability for non-GI applications.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Bioavailability | ~90% |
| Onset | 5–15 minutes |
| Duration | 4–6 hours |
| Frequency | 1–2 times daily |

**Typical research doses:**

| Indication | Dose Range | Frequency |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Tendon/ligament repair | 10–20 µg/kg | 1× daily |
| Muscle injury | 10–20 µg/kg | 1× daily |
| Bone healing | 10–20 µg/kg | 1× daily |
| Neuroprotection | 10–20 µg/kg | 1× daily |

### Injectable Administration (Intraperitoneal)

IP injection is the primary route in preclinical animal studies. Doses are not directly translatable to human research.

| Species | Typical Dose | Frequency |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Rat | 10 µg/kg | 1× daily |
| Mouse | 10 µg/kg | 1× daily |

## Concentration Calculations

### Reconstitution Protocol

**Standard reconstitution:** 5 mg vial + 5 mL bacteriostatic water = 1 mg/mL

| Vial Mass | BAC Water Volume | Final Concentration |
|-----------|------------------|---------------------|
| 5 mg | 5 mL | 1 mg/mL |
| 5 mg | 10 mL | 0.5 mg/mL |
| 5 mg | 2.5 mL | 2 mg/mL |

### Dose Volume Calculation

**Formula:**

```
Volume (mL) = Dose (mg) ÷ Concentration (mg/mL)
```

**Example 1:** 250 µg dose from 1 mg/mL solution:

```
Volume = 0.25 mg ÷ 1 mg/mL = 0.25 mL (25 units on U-100 syringe)
```

**Example 2:** 500 µg dose from 1 mg/mL solution:

```
Volume = 0.5 mg ÷ 1 mg/mL = 0.5 mL (50 units on U-100 syringe)
```

## Injection Technique

### Subcutaneous Administration

1. **Prepare workspace:** Clean surface, sterile supplies
2. **Clean vial stopper:** 70% isopyl alcohol swab
3. **Draw dose:** Use 1 mL syringe with 25–30 gauge needle
4. **Pinch skin:** Create skin fold at injection site
5. **Insert needle:** 45–90 degree angle
6. **Inject slowly:** Over 5–10 seconds
7. **Withdraw needle:** Release skin fold
8. **Apply pressure:** Brief gauze compression

### Preferred Injection Sites

| Site | Absorption Rate | Comfort |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Abdomen (2 inches from navel) | Fastest | Moderate |
| Thigh | Moderate | High |
| Upper arm | Moderate | Moderate |
| Buttock | Slowest | High |

Rotate injection sites to prevent lipodystrophy.

## Preclinical Evidence Summary

| Model | Route | Dose | Outcome |
|-------|-------|------|---------|
| Rat Achilles tendon | SC | 10 µg/kg/day | Enhanced healing, increased biomechanical strength |
| Rat colitis | Oral | 10 µg/kg/day | Reduced inflammation, restored mucosa |
| Rat brain injury | IP | 10 µg/kg/day | Improved neurological recovery |
| Rabbit ligament | SC | 10 µg/kg/day | Accelerated collagen maturation |
| Rat bone fracture | SC | 10 µg/kg/day | Enhanced callus formation |

## Safety Profile

**Preclinical observations:**

- No adverse effects reported at doses up to 500 µg/kg in rats
- No genotoxicity, mutagenicity, or carcinogenicity observed
- No significant organ toxicity in 28-day repeated dose studies
- Gastric acid stability does not cause GI irritation

**Limitations:**

- No human clinical trial data
- Regulatory status: Investigational research chemical
- Not approved for human consumption or medical use
