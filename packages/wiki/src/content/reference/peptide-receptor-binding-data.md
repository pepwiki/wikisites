## Overview

Receptor binding data characterizes the affinity, selectivity, and functional activity of peptide therapeutics at their target receptors. This reference compiles Kd, Ki, EC50, IC50, and selectivity ratios for major peptide-receptor interactions, organized by receptor family.

## Key Parameters

- **Kd (Dissociation constant)**: Concentration at which 50% of receptors are occupied (lower = higher affinity)
- **Ki (Inhibition constant)**: Kd derived from competition binding assays
- **EC50 (Half-maximal effective concentration)**: Concentration producing 50% of maximal response
- **IC50 (Half-maximal inhibitory concentration)**: Concentration producing 50% inhibition
- **Emax (Maximal efficacy)**: Maximum response relative to native ligand (100%)
- **Selectivity ratio**: Fold-difference in affinity between receptor subtypes

## Selectivity Ratios (Clinical Relevance)

| Therapeutic Goal | Desired Selectivity | Consequence of Off-Target |
|------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| GLP-1R-selective | GLP-1R >> GIPR (semaglutide) | GI side effects |
| Dual GLP-1R/GIPR | GLP-1R ≈ GIPR (tirzepatide) | Enhanced metabolic effects |
| MC4R-selective (obesity) | MC4R >> MC1R (setmelanotide) | Sexual function without tanning |
| MC4R/MC1R (sexual) | MC4R > MC1R (bremelanotide) | Sexual effects with minimal tanning |
| V2-selective (antidiuresis) | V2R >> V1aR (desmopressin) | No vasoconstriction |
| SSTR2-selective (NET) | SSTR2 >> SSTR5 (octreotide) | Hormone suppression |
| MOR-selective (analgesia) | MOR >> DOR/KOR | Analgesia with less dysphoria |
