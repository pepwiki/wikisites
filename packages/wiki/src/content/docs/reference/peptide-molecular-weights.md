## Molecular Weight Calculation

The molecular weight of a linear peptide is calculated as:

**MW = Σ MW(residues) − (n − 1) × 18.015**

Where:
- Σ MW(residues) = sum of amino acid residue molecular weights
- n = number of residues
- 18.015 Da = mass of water lost per peptide bond (condensation reaction)

For disulfide-bonded peptides, subtract 2.016 Da per disulfide bond (loss of 2 hydrogen atoms).
