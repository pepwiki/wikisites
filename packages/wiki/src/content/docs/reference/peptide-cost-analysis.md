## Cost Drivers

1. **Raw materials**: Amino acids, reagents, solvents
2. **Manufacturing**: SPPS labor, HPLC purification
3. **Quality control**: Analytical testing per ICH guidelines
4. **Regulatory**: Clinical trials, FDA approval
5. **Distribution**: Cold chain, specialty pharmacy
6. **Intellectual property**: Patent protection periods
7. **Market dynamics**: Payer negotiations, rebates
