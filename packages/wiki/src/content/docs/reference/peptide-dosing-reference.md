### GLP-1 Titration Rules

1. **Start low, go slow** — 4-week minimum at each dose level
2. **GI tolerance** — advance only if nausea is manageable (< moderate)
3. **Glucose monitoring** — check fasting glucose weekly during titration
4. **Maximum efficacy** — higher doses produce greater weight loss and HbA1c reduction
5. **Missed dose** — if missed, resume usual schedule (no dose doubling)

### Insulin Titration Rules

1. **Fasting glucose target**: 80–130 mg/dL
2. **Titration**: Adjust basal insulin by 2 units every 3 days
3. **Self-titration**: Patients may adjust 1–2 units daily based on fasting glucose
4. **Correction factor**: 1 unit lowers glucose by 30–50 mg/dL (individualized)
5. **Carb ratio**: 1 unit per 10–15 g carbohydrate (individualized)

### GH Secretagogue Titration

1. **Start low**: Begin at the lowest effective dose
2. **Fasting requirements**: Administer on empty stomach (ipamorelin, GHRP-6)
3. **IGF-1 monitoring**: Check IGF-1 levels at 4 and 8 weeks
4. **Target IGF-1**: 150–300 ng/mL (age-adjusted)
5. **Dose escalation**: Increase by 50–100% every 4 weeks if IGF-1 is subtherapeutic

## Dose Adjustment Special Populations

### Renal Impairment

| eGFR (mL/min) | Adjustment | Peptides Affected |
|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| >50 | No adjustment | Most peptides |
| 30–50 | Reduce 25% | GLP-1 RAs, insulin |
| 15–30 | Reduce 50% | GLP-1 RAs, insulin |
| <15 or dialysis | Avoid or reduce 75% | Most peptides |

### Hepatic Impairment

| Child-Pugh | Adjustment | Peptides Affected |
|------------|------------|-------------------|
| A (mild) | No adjustment | Most peptides |
| B (moderate) | Reduce 25–50% | GLP-1 RAs |
| C (severe) | Avoid | GLP-1 RAs, GnRH analogs |

### Elderly (>65 years)

- **Start at lowest dose** for all peptide classes
- **Slower titration** — extend titration intervals to 2–4 weeks
- **Monitor renal function** — eGFR may decline with age
- **Lower maximum doses** — reduced lean body mass and clearance
