## GLP-1 Agonist Comparison

## Mechanism of Action

### GLP-1 Receptor Agonists

All agents in this class share the core mechanism:

1. **Pancreatic effects:** Glucose-dependent insulin secretion, glucagon suppression
2. **GI effects:** Delayed gastric emptying, satiety signaling
3. **Central effects:** Appetite suppression, reward pathway modulation
4. **Cardiovascular:** Reduced MACE in high-risk populations

### Dual GIP/GLP-1 Agonists (Tirzepatide)

Tirzepatide activates both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, producing enhanced metabolic effects through dual incretin signaling.

## Selection Framework

### Prioritize HbA1c Reduction

1. Tirzepatide 15 mg (−1.6 to −2.0%)
2. Semaglutide 2.0 mg (−1.5 to −1.8%)
3. Semaglutide 1.0 mg (−1.3 to −1.6%)

### Prioritize Weight Loss

1. Tirzepatide 15 mg (−15 to −21%)
2. Semaglutide 2.4 mg (−12 to −15%)
3. Liraglutide 3.0 mg (−5 to −8%)

### Prioritize CV Protection

1. Semaglutide (strongest SUSTAIN-6 data)
2. Liraglutide (LEADER trial)
3. Dulaglutide (REWIND trial)

### Prioritize Cost/Access

1. Exenatide (generic availability)
2. Dulaglutide (biosimilar development)
3. Semaglutide (Wegovy savings cards)
